## What is Hosting?

**Hosting** refers to the service of storing and serving websites or web applications on the internet. A hosting provider allocates space on a server for your website files, making them accessible to users worldwide.

### Real World Example

Think of hosting like renting an apartment. Your website is your furniture and belongings, and the hosting provider is the landlord who rents you space in their building (the server). Just as people visit your apartment to see your things, users visit your website by accessing the server where your site is hosted.

## Types of Hosting

1. **Shared Hosting**  
    Multiple websites share the same server resources. It's cost-effective but may have limited performance and security.

2. **Dedicated Hosting**  
    You rent an entire server for your website. This offers better performance, control, and security, but is more expensive.

3. **Managed Hosting**  
    The hosting provider manages the server, including maintenance, updates, and security, allowing you to focus on your website.

## Ways of Hosting

- **On-Premises Hosting:** Hosting your website on your own physical servers.
- **Cloud Hosting:** Using cloud providers (like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud) to host your website on virtual servers.
- **VPS Hosting:** Virtual Private Server hosting splits a physical server into multiple virtual servers, offering more control than shared hosting.
- **Colocation:** Renting space in a data center to place your own server hardware.
